## Documented basis

The project implements observed behavior in its own React/CSS, Python and native host code. Its documented method excludes proprietary Hudiy implementation code, binaries and extracted assets. It uses a separately licensed open-source Android Auto protocol library and records the upstream native adaptation. It does not claim that all code is original or MIT-only.

The observation exception and functionality/expression distinction are explained with primary Austrian statutes and CJEU judgments in the assessment. They do not settle the protection of a distinctive GUI, original documentation, branding or third-party assets. Artcraft's public approach is a comparison, not a legal precedent approving coscarOS.
